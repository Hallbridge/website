FAQ

Construction recruiting,
answered.

The questions hiring managers and candidates ask us most. Anything else, just ask.

What does Hallbridge Talent Partners do?

Hallbridge is a construction executive search and recruiting firm. We place project managers, superintendents, project engineers, estimators, preconstruction leaders and construction executives with general contractors, multifamily developers and engineering firms across the United States.

Which construction roles do you recruit for?

Field leadership, including superintendents, assistant superintendents, project managers, project engineers and assistant field staff. Office and executive roles, including vice presidents, directors and project executives, preconstruction managers, estimators and civil engineers.

What types of projects do your candidates build?

High-rise, Class A multifamily, student housing, mixed-use and commercial construction, typically on projects from $20M to over $100M.

Where does Hallbridge recruit?

Nationwide. We have placed construction professionals in states including Massachusetts, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Virginia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Florida, Tennessee, Indiana, Illinois, Texas, Colorado, Utah, Arizona, Nevada, California, Oregon, Washington and Hawaii.

How are search fees structured?

Contingency search has no upfront cost: you pay only when we place someone. Retained search is available for critical leadership hires that need dedicated focus and exclusivity. Fees depend on the role and the type of search, so reach out for terms.

Do candidates pay anything to work with Hallbridge?

No. Hallbridge is paid by hiring companies, never by candidates, and every candidate conversation is confidential.

How quickly will we see candidates?

Clients typically see qualified, vetted candidates within a few days to a week of starting a search.

How do we start a search?

Email info@hallbridge.co with the role, the location and your timing. The first conversation is free and confidential.
