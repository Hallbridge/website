Manufacturing & Industrial

Show buyers what your shop can make.

Engineers, estimators and purchasing teams look at a supplier's website before they ever send a drawing. We build websites that put your capabilities, equipment and quality standards up front, so the right buyers see a shop they can trust with the work.

Request a Website Assessment See how a concept works ↓

The Gap

Strong work, quiet websites.

Most small manufacturers win work through referrals and reputation, and the website never kept pace with the shop. None of this reflects the quality of the work. It just makes it harder for a new buyer to choose you.

  • Capabilities summed up in a single line, when buyers search for a specific process
  • An equipment list that is years out of date, or missing
  • Certifications mentioned but never shown
  • No simple way to send a drawing or request a quote
  • Photos that miss the floor, the machines and the finished product
  • A site that falls apart on the phone a buyer is holding

02 · Proof

Everything they look for before the RFQ.

A manufacturer's website has a different job than a brochure. It has to answer technical questions quickly and prove the shop is real.

  • Capabilities by ProcessA page for each process you run, so buyers land on exactly what they need.
  • Equipment & MachineryMake, model, size and capacity, kept current as the shop grows.
  • Materials & TolerancesWhat you work with and the standards you hold.
  • Certifications & QualityAISC, PCI, AWI, AWS, ISO and the inspection behind them, shown clearly.
  • Product & Project GalleriesReal photos of fabricated work, installed and ready to ship.

04 · Search

Found when engineers go looking.

Buyers search for a process, a material or a certification, rarely a company name. We structure every page so search engines and AI assistants understand exactly what you make and where.

  • Search FoundationsClean structure, fast pages and a distinct title for every process page.
  • AI Search ReadinessCapabilities, equipment and certifications written as plain facts that AI tools can read and cite.
  • Local PresenceGoogle Business Profile and directory listings, for shops that serve customers nearby.

05 · RFQ

From drawing to quote, faster.

A quote form that collects what your estimator needs the first time and routes it to the right person.

  • Drawing upload: PDF, DWG, DXF or STEP
  • Quantities, material, finish and due date
  • Routed to the right estimator, with a confirmation to the buyer
  • Secure transfer for large or controlled drawings, instead of email
