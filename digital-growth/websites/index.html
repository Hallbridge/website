Contractor Websites

The work is the pitch. Let the site show it.

Hallbridge is a construction recruiting firm that knows the trades, so we build contractor websites that put your projects, your people and your qualifications in front of the owners, general contractors and job seekers who look you up before they call.

Request a Website Concept Or start with a free check of your current site ↓

Common Gaps

Why most contractor sites fall short.

We spend our days talking with superintendents, project managers and estimators, and with the leaders who hire them. Both sides judge a company by its website long before the first meeting, and most contractor sites undersell the work behind them.

  1. A dated look and stock photosAn old template and pictures of someone else's crew tell an owner nothing about yours.
  2. Services listed, nothing provenA list of what you do reads like every competitor until a finished job backs it up.
  3. Project work buriedYour best jobs sit deep in a menu, or in a gallery with no location, scope or team attached.
  4. A careers page that costs you hiresStrong candidates check your site before they return a recruiter's call. A single "now hiring" line sends them to a competitor.
  5. Slow on a phoneOwners, supers and job seekers often open your site from a truck or a jobsite. If it stalls, they move on.

Standard Scope

What every Hallbridge site includes.

  • A home page built around the workProjects, markets and people on the first screen, not a slogan over a generic image.
  • A page for each projectScope, location, team and photos for every job, so an owner can find work like theirs.
  • A careers page built to recruitRecruiting is our core business. We write it the way good candidates read: real roles, real crews, a path up and an easy way to apply.
  • Bid and contact forms that reach the right personOwner inquiries, bid invitations and job seekers each land with whoever handles them.
  • Safety and qualifications pageLicenses, certifications, your safety program and the documents a general contractor asks for at prequalification.
  • Set up for Google and for AI searchClean structure, a distinct title on every page and plain facts that search engines and AI assistants can read.
  • Fast on every phoneLean pages and properly sized photos, tested on real devices before launch.
  • Hosting, security, backups and updatesHandled for you, quietly, in the background.
  • One point of contactOne person who knows your company and answers your calls, before and after launch.
  • Fixed scope, agreed up frontYou know exactly what you are getting, and what it costs, before we begin.
  • Changes after launch, based on real visitorsWe watch how people actually use the site and adjust the parts they miss.
  • You own itYour domain, your words, your photos and the finished site belong to your company.

FAQ

Questions contractors ask.

Straight answers to what we hear most. Anything else, email us.

How long does a new website take?

A few weeks once we have your photos and details. Gathering project information usually sets the pace, and we do most of that legwork with you in a short conversation.

What does a website cost?

It depends on the pages and features you need, so we agree a fixed scope and price before any work starts. Half is due to begin and the balance at launch. There are no surprise change orders.

Do you work with specialty contractors?

Yes. Electrical, mechanical, concrete, steel, roofing, site work and other trades. A trade contractor's site has its own job: getting you invited to bid by the general contractors you want to work for.

Will we keep our Google rankings?

We plan for it. Before launch we map every existing page to its new home and set up redirects, keep the titles and content that already rank, and connect Google Search Console to watch for problems. Rankings can shift for a short time after any rebuild, and we watch closely through that period.

Who updates the site after launch?

We do. Send new projects, photos, job openings or edits by email and we make the updates, so your team never has to learn a website tool.

Are you based in the US?

Yes. Hallbridge is a US company working with construction companies nationwide. You can reach us at (813) 850-0583 or info@hallbridge.co.

What if our current site is not broken?

Then it may only need a few changes, and we will tell you so. The free check shows where it stands today and what, if anything, is worth changing. If a full rebuild is not worth it, we will say that too.

Do we own the site?

Yes. Everything we build for you is yours, including the domain and the content. If you ever decide to move on, we hand it all over.
