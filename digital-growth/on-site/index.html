Home  /  Digital Growth  /  On-Site Content Days

Your work, captured where it happens.

We come to your project sites or office and capture professional photos, video and content of your projects, crews and leadership, ready for your website, proposals, recruiting and LinkedIn.

What We Capture

Every side of your business.

  • Projects in progress and finished
  • Crews at work, in PPE and on site rules
  • Leadership and team headshots
  • Aerial progress shots, where site and FAA rules allow
  • Short video: project walk-throughs, owner messages, recruiting clips
  • Your office and culture

How It Works

One visit. No slowdowns.

01

Plan

A short call to pick the sites, build a shot list and time the visit around the moments worth capturing. We coordinate access and safety with your superintendent.

02

Capture

We work around your crews and follow every site rule, so the job keeps moving while we shoot.

03

Deliver

Edited, color-graded photos and clips in a shared folder, sized for your website, proposals, print and social.

Where It Goes

Content that gets used.

Every set comes with a short note on where each shot works best, so nothing sits in a folder.

  • Website project pages and portfolio
  • Proposals and bid packages
  • Careers page and job posts
  • LinkedIn and Google Business Profile
