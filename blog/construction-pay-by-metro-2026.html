Construction pay by metro: 30 markets ranked.

By Alex Hall · November 3, 2026

Where you build changes what you pay. We ranked 30 metros by the median base pay general contractors and developer-builders posted for project managers and superintendents in 2026. Every metro on the list had at least 10 postings for each role.

What stands out

  • The San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles and New York and New Jersey lead the list, with medians from $154,000 to $167,000 for both roles.
  • Phoenix and Austin punch above their weight. Phoenix project managers post a $150,000 median and Austin superintendents post $152,000, both on par with coastal markets.
  • The Mid-Atlantic is the value market. Washington DC and Baltimore project managers post a $120,000 median, among the lowest on the list, even though both are busy construction markets.

Base pay is only part of the offer. In most of these markets, vehicle, per diem and bonus can move the total well beyond the numbers above, especially for superintendents.
