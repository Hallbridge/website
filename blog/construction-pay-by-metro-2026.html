Construction pay by metro: where it is highest in 2026.

By Alex Hall · November 3, 2026

Where you build changes what you pay. Here is how the major metros group for project manager and superintendent base pay this year.

The top tier: $150K and up

The San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles and the New York and New Jersey market lead the country for both roles, with Orange County and San Diego close behind.

The next tier: about $135K to $150K

Phoenix, Austin, Dallas-Fort Worth, Sacramento and Orlando punch above their weight. In some of these markets, project managers or superintendents are offered as much as their coastal peers.

Most other markets: about $120K to $135K

Chicago, Atlanta, Charlotte, Nashville, Denver, much of Florida and most of the Midwest fall here. The Mid-Atlantic, including Washington DC and Baltimore, sits toward the lower end despite being a busy construction market.

Rounded base salary for project managers and superintendents. Bonus, vehicle and per diem are not included.

Winning in a tight market

In the top markets, every builder is paying well. The companies that win the best people are the ones candidates already know and trust: a current website, recent projects, real photos of the team and a careers page that makes the next step easy. Pay gets you in the conversation. Your reputation, online and off, closes it.
