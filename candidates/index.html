The Process

How it works for you.

Bridging the gap between where you are and what you’ll build next.

01

A real conversation

We learn what you build, what you want next and what would make a move worth it.

02

Your call, every time

Free Resume Review

A second set of eyes, from someone who hires.

Send your resume and we will tell you how it reads to a construction hiring manager: what stands out, what gets missed and what to change. Free, with no obligation to work with us.

  • Does it show the size and type of projects you have run?
  • Are your results clear, not just your duties?
  • Would a busy superintendent or VP read past the first half page?
