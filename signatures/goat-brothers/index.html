How to set it up in Gmail

  1. Open this page in Chrome on your computer and pick the signature you like.
  2. Click just before the logo, hold, and drag to the end of the last line so the whole signature is highlighted. Copy it (Ctrl+C or Command+C).
  3. In Gmail, click the gear at the top right, then See all settings. On the General tab, scroll down to Signature and click Create new. Name it "Goat Brothers".
  4. Click in the box and paste (Ctrl+V or Command+V).
  5. If the logo doesn't show up after pasting: delete the empty spot, click the image icon in the signature toolbar, choose Upload, and pick the matching logo file attached to the email ("Goat signature logo.png" or, for the black bar, "Goat signature logo white.png"). Click the image and choose Small.
  6. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and for replies and forwards.
  7. Scroll to the very bottom and click Save Changes.
  8. Phone (Gmail app): in the app go to Settings, your account, Mobile signature. The app only does plain text, so type your name, title, Goat Brothers and the number.
  9. Send yourself a test email to check it.
