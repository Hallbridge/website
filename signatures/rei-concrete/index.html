How to set it up

  1. Open this file in Chrome, Edge or Safari, find your signature and pick the option you like.
  2. Click just to the left of the logo, hold the mouse down, and drag to the end of the last line so the whole signature is highlighted. Copy it (Ctrl+C on Windows, Command+C on Mac).
  3. Outlook on a Windows computer: File, then Options, then Mail, then Signatures. Click New, name it "REI", paste into the box (Ctrl+V), then set it as the default for new messages and replies. Click OK.
  4. New Outlook or Outlook on the web: click the gear (Settings) at the top right, then Account (or Mail), then Signatures. Click New signature, paste, choose it for new messages and replies, then Save.
  5. If the logo doesn't come through when you paste: delete the empty box, click the picture icon in the signature editor, and insert the matching image attached to the email ("REI signature logo.png" or "REI signature seal.png"). Then drag its corner until it is about the size shown here.
  6. iPhone: Settings, then Apps, then Mail, then Signature. The iPhone only does plain text, so type your name, title, REI Concrete and your numbers.
  7. Changing details: after pasting, click into the signature and type over anything you want to change.
  8. Send yourself a test email to check it looks right.
